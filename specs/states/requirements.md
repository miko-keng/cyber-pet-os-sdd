# Requirements: Dynamic States

## 1. Normal
- Default state.

## 2. Sick
- **Trigger:** Hunger OR Energy drops to < 10.
- **Penalty:** Cannot use 'Play' action.

## 3. Evolved
- **Trigger:** Happiness, Hunger, and Energy all maintained > 90 for 2 continuous minutes.
- **Benefit:** Decay rates halved.

## Visual Feedback
- **pet_normal.png**: Green phosphor CRT screen with a cute 8-bit pixel cat.
- **pet_sick.png**: Distorted amber CRT screen with 'SYSTEM_FAILURE' text.
- **pet_evolved.png**: Golden CRT screen with winged pixel cat and sunglasses.
